# Quality criteria by category

Use the section for the category. Each one lists what to measure, where real measurements live, and the labels stores fake. If a category isn't here, make a short section like these from what reviewers measure, and add it to this file.

## TVs
- **Picture:** panel (VA = deeper blacks; IPS = wider angles, grey blacks), backlight (edge / direct / Mini LED), **local dimming zone count**, measured HDR peak nits, native contrast, Dolby Vision / HDR10+, reflections, processing.
- **Sound:** watts, channels, subwoofer, Atmos, and what reviewers say about it.
- **Motion:** native refresh rate. 120Hz needs "4K@120 over HDMI 2.1". "Motion Rate", "Motion Booster" and "DLG 120" mean a 60Hz panel.
- **Reviews:** RTINGS, HDTVTest, FlatpanelsHD, What Hi-Fi, TechRadar, Tom's Guide, AVForums.
- **Fake labels:** "Mini LED" with no local dimming (e.g. Samsung M70H/M80H); "OLED" on a QLED; "144Hz" = VRR ceiling or a DLG mode. Regional codes hide reviewed twins. "OLED" in titles of QLED sets (B.TECH Samsung 55LS03H Frame; Raya TCL 55P7K). LG QNED84/85 "Mini LED" is edge-lit. Samsung S85F 55" uses WOLED outside the US. LG CS4/CS5 = regional C-series variant (CS4 has α9 Gen7), no direct review.
- **Spec missing?** In the store, check the HDMI settings for a "4K 120Hz" option, and look at the screen from the side: colours that wash out mean VA.

## Refrigerators
- Net capacity (litres), configuration (top/bottom freezer, side-by-side), cooling (No Frost, dual/twin cooling), **inverter compressor** and its warranty, energy class / measured kWh per year, noise (dB), temperature stability, build (shelves, door seals), and service network in the market.
- **Reviews:** Which?, Consumer Reports summaries, RTINGS (some), Choice, local forums.
- **Fake labels:** "Inverter" claims (check the compressor model); gross vs net litres.

## Washing machines
- Load (kg), type (front/top load), spin rpm, **inverter direct-drive motor**, energy and water per cycle, noise, program time, steam, dryer combo, service network.
- **Reviews:** Which?, Choice, Consumer Reports.
- **Fake labels:** "8 kg" washer-dryer combos that only dry about half the load.

## Air conditioners
- Capacity (HP / BTU) matched to the room size, **inverter**, cooling-only vs heat/cool, EER/SEER, noise (indoor dB), refrigerant (R32/R410A), copper vs aluminium coil, warranty on the compressor, installation cost.
- **Fake labels:** "Inverter-ready", and capacity rounded up.

## Phones
- Chipset (performance and efficiency), camera (main sensor size, OIS, real samples), display (OLED, brightness nits, refresh), battery (mAh plus a **measured** endurance test), charging, software update years, build/IP rating, local warranty vs imported.
- **Reviews:** GSMArena (battery and display tests), NotebookCheck, DXOMARK (with caution), MKBHD/Mrwhosetheboss for real use.
- **Fake labels:** imported/grey units sold as "official"; RAM "extended" with storage.

## Laptops
- CPU/GPU (performance at the actual wattage), RAM (upgradable?), SSD, display (panel, brightness, sRGB coverage, refresh), battery (measured), weight, keyboard, thermals and noise, ports, warranty.
- **Reviews:** NotebookCheck (measured everything), RTINGS laptops, LaptopMedia.
- **Fake labels:** "RTX 4060" at a low TGP; "FHD IPS" with 45% NTSC.

## Furniture (tables, desks, sofas, beds)
- Dimensions vs the room, material (solid wood / MDF / particle board / metal gauge), finish, load rating, joinery and hardware, assembly, delivery and returns, warranty, real customer photos.
- **Reviews:** store reviews with photos, Facebook groups, in-person checks.
- **Fake labels:** "wood" meaning MDF or chipboard; renders instead of real photos.
